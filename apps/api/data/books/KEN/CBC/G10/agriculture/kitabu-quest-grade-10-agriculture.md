## Land Preparation Land clearing Primary cultivation Secondary cultivation Tertiary operations. Conservation tillage: Worked Example

Practical model for Land Preparation Land clearing Primary cultivation Secondary cultivation Tertiary operations. Conservation tillage: Water-use plan for a garden bed.

Procedure:
1. Check soil moisture before watering by observing colour and feel.
2. Decide whether the bed needs watering, mulching, drainage, or shade.
3. Water near the root zone and avoid wasting water on paths.
4. Record amount, time, weather, and plant response.
5. Suggest one conservation action such as mulching, watering early, fixing leaks, or using a watering can with a rose.

Hazards: slippery paths, contaminated water, heavy containers, and flooded roots.

Rubric: excellent work links water action to evidence, avoids waste, protects plants, and keeps a useful record.

## Beekeeping: explain the factors to consider in siting: Worked Example

Practical model for Beekeeping: explain the factors to consider in siting: Beekeeping safety and observation.

Teacher-supervised procedure:
1. Wear protective clothing: veil, gloves, long sleeves, trousers, and closed shoes.
2. Approach the hive calmly from the side or back, not directly in front of the entrance.
3. Use smoke only under trained supervision to calm bees.
4. Inspect quickly for comb condition, pests, and signs of honey, then close the hive gently.
5. Keep learners with allergies or fear of stings away from the hive area.

Hazards: stings, allergic reactions, fire from smoker, and disturbing bees.

Quality criteria: safe distance, correct protective clothing, calm movement, clear observation record, and no damage to the colony.
