## Listening and Speaking: Worked Example

Listening model for Listening and Speaking: Catch key details from a recording or teacher-read text.

Transcript excerpt: "At dawn, Naliaka and her brother carried seedlings to the school garden. Their teacher reminded the club to water the young trees before the sun became hot. By Friday, every group had labelled its row and recorded the number of seedlings planted."

Question: Which details should a listener record?

Model answer:
1. Who: Naliaka, her brother, the teacher, and the club.
2. Where: the school garden.
3. What happened: seedlings were planted, watered, labelled, and recorded.
4. Message: careful listening helps us retell events in order.

Grade 5 check: A strong listening answer names people, place, event order, and message without inventing details.
