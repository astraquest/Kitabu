## Point, lines and geometric shapes in 2D: Worked Example

Worked example for Point, lines and geometric shapes in 2D: Classify a shape with 4 equal sides and 4 right angles.

1. Four sides means it is a quadrilateral.
2. Equal sides and right angles match a square.
3. Check the name and properties.

Answer: The shape is a square.

## SEQUENCES: Worked Example

Worked example for SEQUENCES: Continue the pattern 6, 12, 18, 24, __, __.

1. Compare terms: 12 - 6 = 6, 18 - 12 = 6.
2. Rule: add 6 each time.
3. 24 + 6 = 30, 30 + 6 = 36.

Answer: 30, 36.
