## Mechanics Momentum And Energy Review: Learn

Mechanics Momentum And Energy Review connects work, power, and energy changes.

Core idea:
- Work is done when a force moves an object through a distance.
- Energy is the ability to do work.
- Power describes how quickly work is done or energy is transferred.
- Energy can change form, such as chemical to movement, electrical to light, or potential to kinetic.

## Mechanics Momentum And Energy Review: Worked Example

Worked example for Mechanics Momentum And Energy Review: A learner lifts a book from the floor to a desk.

1. The learner applies an upward force.
2. The book moves upward through a distance.
3. Work is done on the book.
4. The book gains gravitational potential energy.

Conclusion: work can transfer energy to an object.

## Electricity Magnetism And Applications: Learn

Electricity Magnetism And Applications explains magnetic and non-magnetic materials.

Core idea:
- Magnets attract some materials, especially iron and steel.
- A magnet has north and south poles.
- Like poles repel and unlike poles attract.
- Magnetic force can act without touching the object.

- Heat moves from a hotter object to a cooler object.
- Thermometers must be read carefully and safely.

## Modern Physics And Radiation Safety: Worked Example

Investigation model for Modern Physics And Radiation Safety: Measure the length of a desk safely.
