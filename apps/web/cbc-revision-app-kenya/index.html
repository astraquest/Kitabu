CBC revision

The CBC revision app Kenya’s parents have been asking for.

Revision only works when it matches what the teacher taught. Kitabu AI follows the CBC syllabus strand by strand — and shows you, the parent, that the revision is actually working.

Why CBC revision is different

The CBC rewards understanding, practice, and application — not cramming. That’s good news for learners, but hard on parents: the methods aren’t the ones we studied, the terms are new, and a revision book can’t answer a question. Many families buy more books, add tuition, and still meet a surprise on the report card, because the gap was never visible in time.

What a good CBC revision app should do

  • Follow the syllabus, strand by strand. Revision that contradicts the classroom confuses a child. Kitabu’s lessons and quizzes mirror the CBC structure — see how alignment works.
  • Start at the real level. A free ≈15-minute check-up finds where your child actually is — even if a Grade 8 learner is stuck on Grade 6 fractions — and builds up from there.
  • Explain patiently, in English or Kiswahili. Your child asks by typing or talking, and can ask again and again. No judgment, no rushing.
  • Show the parent. A daily dashboard plus a Friday WhatsApp message: what was studied, what improved, what still needs help.
  • Work on real Kenyan phones. Downloaded books and saved lessons work offline; the live tutor needs a connection.
