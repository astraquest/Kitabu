Curriculum

Built strand by strand for the CBC (CBE) syllabus.

Kitabu AI follows the same learning areas, strands, and sub-strands your child’s teacher follows — so evening revision continues the classroom, instead of contradicting it.

How alignment works

Every lesson, explanation, and quiz in Kitabu is organised by learning area, strand, and sub-strand, mirroring the CBC (CBE) structure used in Kenyan classrooms. When your child asks a question, the tutor answers it "the CBC way" — using the terms, methods, and presentation their teacher expects, in English or Kiswahili.

Current coverage

  • Upper primary (Grades 4–6): Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, Science & Technology, Social Studies, Agriculture & Nutrition, Creative Arts, and Religious Education.
  • Junior school (Grades 7–9): Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, Integrated Science, Social Studies, Pre-Technical Studies, Agriculture & Nutrition, Creative Arts & Sports, and Religious Education.
  • Grade 10: senior-school subjects are rolling out, expanding to full Senior School coverage.

Exam preparation follows the national assessments learners actually face: KPSEA at Grade 6 and KJSEA at junior school, with a bridge for learners heading toward KCSE-track content.

Honest claims, always

Kitabu AI is CBC-aligned: our content is built to follow the CBC (CBE) syllabus structure. We do not claim KICD approval, and we won’t until a formal approval is in hand. Where content is still rolling out (Senior School), we say so rather than overselling.

A diagnostic before anything else

Alignment isn’t only about content — it’s about level. Kitabu starts every learner with a free ≈15-minute check-up that finds their real starting point, even if a Grade 8 learner needs Grade 6 fractions first. The path up is built from there.
