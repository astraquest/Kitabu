Account support

Account Deletion Request

Kitabu AI lets eligible users request account deletion from inside the app. Account deletion requests are fulfilled in 30 days, and account data is deleted from our servers.

Request deletion in the app

Open Kitabu AI, sign in, go to your profile, then choose Account deletion. To prevent accidental requests, the app asks you to review the warning and type a confirmation phrase before the request is submitted.

After the request is submitted, you are signed out and the account is marked for deletion. The account cannot continue using Kitabu AI while deletion is pending.

If you cannot access the app

Email hello@kitabu.ai from the email address linked to your Kitabu AI account. Include the account email or phone number, your name, and the role on the account, such as student, parent, or teacher.

We may ask for additional information to verify that the request is from the account owner or an authorized guardian, parent, school representative, or administrator.

What gets deleted

Account deletion removes the account and associated account data from Kitabu AI servers, including profile details, learning activity, progress records, saved preferences, and account-linked service records that are no longer needed.

Some limited records may be retained only where required for security, fraud prevention, dispute handling, legal obligations, or financial records.

School-managed accounts

If your account is managed by a school or organization, we may need to coordinate the request with the school administrator or authorized account owner before deletion is completed.
