Holidays

Holiday revision at home, on your terms.

Holidays are for resting — and for keeping hard-won skills warm. Kitabu makes parent-led home revision short, calm, and visible.

The holiday dilemma

Every parent knows the shape of it: a long holiday, a phone in hand, and the quiet worry that Term 1’s progress is melting. At the same time, children genuinely need the rest. The answer isn’t an eight-hour timetable — it’s short, regular, parent-led home revision that protects both the learning and the holiday.

A holiday rhythm that works

  • Twenty minutes, most mornings. One topic, one explanation, one quiz. Then the day is theirs.
  • Repair season. Holidays are the best time to fix foundations without the pressure of new classwork — the diagnostic finds what needs repairing.
  • You decide the pace. This is parent-led home revision: you choose the days, the subjects, and the intensity. Kitabu just makes each minute count.
  • Offline where needed. Downloaded books and saved lessons travel to shags with you — no bundles needed for reading. The live tutor needs a connection.

And when school reopens

The learner who kept skills warm starts the term ahead instead of recovering. You’ll see it on your Friday WhatsApp updates through the holiday — what was practised, what improved. Common questions

Holiday revision, answered.

How much revision should my child do in the holidays?

Short and regular beats long and rare: around 20 focused minutes most days keeps skills warm and leaves the holiday intact. You set the pace.

Can we revise while travelling upcountry?

Downloaded books and saved lessons work offline wherever you are. The live tutor needs a connection when questions come up.

Is this the same as holiday classes?

No — this is parent-led home revision, on your terms, at home. You stay in charge of your child’s holiday.
