Is Kitabu AI on the App Store and Google Play?

Almost — we're in launch preparation. Tap any button to get early access on WhatsApp today, and we'll notify you the day the stores go live.

Which learners is Kitabu built for?

Kitabu is built for Kenyan learners in Grade 4–10 at launch, expanding to Senior School. Lessons follow the CBC syllabus, in English and Kiswahili.

Does it replace teachers?

No. Kitabu gives learners a second, patient explanation after school and takes on less of the routine marking — teachers review and adjust. The teacher stays the teacher.

How do I pay?

With M-Pesa. Start with the free diagnostic — no card needed — and pay from KSh 250 per month, per learner, only when you're ready.

Does it work without internet?

You can download books and saved lessons for offline revision. The live tutor needs a connection.

Is it safe for my child?

Kitabu is a guided, age-appropriate learning space built for schoolwork — not random scrolling. We follow the Kenya Data Protection Act, ask for parent consent for learners under 18, and collect only what's needed for learning. More on safety →
