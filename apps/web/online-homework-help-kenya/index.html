Homework help

Online homework help in Kenya that teaches — not copy-paste.

The point of homework help isn’t finishing the exercise. It’s a child who could do the next one alone. That’s the difference Kitabu is built around.

The 8 PM problem

It’s evening. The homework is open. The child is stuck, the parent is tired — or at work, or in another town — and the textbook explains it in exactly the way that didn’t work the first time. Someone in the WhatsApp group shares an answer, the exercise gets finished, and nothing was learned. Next week, the same topic returns on a test.

Help that leaves understanding behind

  • Step by step, never just the answer. Kitabu walks the working line by line, the CBC way, and checks understanding before moving on.
  • Ask again. And again. By typing or talking, in English or Kiswahili — no judgment, no laughing, no rushing.
  • Practice follows help. After the explanation comes a short quiz on the same idea, so tonight’s help becomes next week’s independence.
  • Parents see the pattern. If homework keeps snagging on the same strand, your Friday WhatsApp update names it — before it becomes a wall.
