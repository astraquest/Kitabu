Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 19, 2026

Kitabu AI provides educational software for students, parents, teachers, school administrators, and platform administrators. We collect and use only the information reasonably needed to create accounts, deliver educational features, manage subscriptions, and keep the service secure.

Information We Collect

This can include your full name, email address, phone number, password hash, account role, school and class relationships, grade level, profile preferences, assignments, submissions, progress, quiz and game activity, subscription records, payment status, AI usage data, device or session metadata, and security logs.

How We Use Information

We use data to create and secure accounts, deliver learning content, personalize educational progress, enforce role-based access, support parents and schools, manage subscriptions, measure AI usage against plan limits, investigate misuse, and improve performance and reliability.

Role-Based Access

Teachers can access only the classes and students linked to them. Parents can access linked child progress where the relationship is verified. School administrators can access only their own school data. Platform administrators can access platform-wide operational data needed to run, support, and secure the service.

AI Features

When AI-supported features are used, we may process prompts, relevant learning context, model metadata, token counts, estimated cost, and timestamps. AI output may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be reviewed where appropriate.

Payments and M-Pesa

For subscription checkout, we may store billing status, plan details, payment request status, and an M-Pesa phone number where needed to start or recover a checkout. We do not store M-Pesa PINs.

Sharing and Processors

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate the app, such as hosting, authentication, email, analytics, AI providers, payment processing, security monitoring, and customer support. These providers may process data only for service delivery and security purposes.

Children and School Use

Kitabu AI is designed for education. Where schools or parents manage learner access, they are responsible for ensuring learners use the service with appropriate permission and supervision.

Retention and Deletion

We keep data only for as long as reasonably necessary for service delivery, school operations, subscription records, security, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Eligible users can request account deletion from the in-app profile or follow the instructions on our account deletion request page. Account deletion requests are fulfilled in 30 days, and account data is deleted from our servers.

Security

We use password hashing, token-based authentication, role-based access controls, secure storage practices, and audit logging for sensitive actions. No system is perfectly secure, so users should protect their credentials and report suspicious activity promptly.

Contact

Privacy questions and support requests may be sent to hello@kitabu.ai.
