# Grade 6 reusable 3D asset production backlog

This is a ranked backlog of 100 **grade-neutral** reusable assets. Grade 6 KICD references identify the first planned scene only; the asset code, model and semantic parts must not contain grade, lesson, question or learner copy.

## Evidence and ranking

The reviewed KICD plans are the mapping source. Official previews:

- Science and Technology: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Cqoxx-afRo1d3DdjdCY8l5STD1lXJhJI/preview
- Mathematics: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ki1N1YnslIpZomG-0IoYogkzek7CKx0j/preview
- Social Studies: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1H1QZ6wgFPsEL6S4A7dFdjukr2jEG8sS2/preview
- English: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1QR9nW3baakrHLIIpv-9UfQyMbOoDTcDX/preview
- Kiswahili: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1p4DSwvmGPzn3ZHCZhRTN88Zvju3XtgYf/preview

Each item is scored 0–3 for curriculum coverage, cross-grade/subject reuse, 3D learning benefit and feasibility/mobile cost; total is out of 12. Ties resolve by 3D benefit, feasibility, reuse, then the current curriculum-coverage gap. Waves contain six items, except final Wave 17 (four items). The canonical machine-readable list is [grade-6-production-backlog.json](../../learning-assets/grade-6-production-backlog.json).

Language mappings only select reusable physical/contextual scenes where spatial context contributes; grammar, vocabulary and writing-only concepts are not represented as standalone 3D assets.
