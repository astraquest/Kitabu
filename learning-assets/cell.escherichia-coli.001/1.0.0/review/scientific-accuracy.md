# Escherichia coli Cell Scientific Accuracy Review

## Decision

**Scientific contract approved for production, with conditional final acceptance.** The specification is appropriate for a representative vegetative *Escherichia coli* cell if the final asset preserves the Gram-negative envelope, presents the nucleoid as non-membrane-bounded, and labels plasmids and surface appendages as optional. Final approval still requires inspection of the rendered geometry, cutaway behavior, labels, and all-around evidence.

This review evaluates the scientific specification, not a completed model. No final factory, turntable, hotspot anchors, or mobile render was available in this review scope on 2026-07-27.

## Required biological reading

The cell body should read as a volumetric rod with rounded poles. Approximately 2–3 µm long and 0.8–1.0 µm wide is a defensible representative teaching proportion, but it is not a species-wide constant. Size and aspect ratio change with strain, nutrition, growth rate, cell-cycle stage, and experimental conditions.

The most important cutaway relationship is the envelope order. From outside inward, the viewer must resolve **outer membrane with LPS in its outer leaflet → periplasm containing a thin peptidoglycan sacculus → inner membrane → cytoplasm**. The peptidoglycan cannot be drawn as a thick Gram-positive wall, placed outside the outer membrane, or merged with either membrane. The periplasm is a real compartment between the two membranes, not cytoplasm or extracellular empty space.

The chromosomal DNA belongs in a **nucleoid**, a condensed and dynamically organized DNA/RNA/protein region that has no enclosing membrane. It should not resemble a eukaryotic nucleus, nucleolus, or a cluster of X-shaped chromosomes. Ribosomes are numerous cytoplasmic 70S particles. Experiments show partial spatial segregation between dense nucleoid regions and ribosome-rich cytoplasm, but that organization changes with growth and perturbation; the model must not imply a universal fixed ribosome lattice.

Plasmids are optional extrachromosomal DNA. A cell with no visible plasmid is scientifically valid. If plasmids are shown, their visible size and separation are didactic exaggerations and no exact copy number or gene cargo should be claimed without a named plasmid-bearing strain.

Flagella, fimbriae, and conjugative pili must also be optional. Flagellated motile *E. coli* use long helical filaments and may have several per cell, but number and expression vary. Fimbriae are shorter adhesive fibers and can be phase-variable. A conjugative pilus requires relevant conjugation machinery, often encoded by a plasmid; it must not be presented as a universal part of the species. The terms pili and fimbriae overlap in parts of the literature, so a lesson that shows both should distinguish the modeled adhesive and conjugative systems by function as well as shape.

The asset must contain **no nucleus, nuclear envelope, nucleolus, mitochondria, Golgi apparatus, endoplasmic reticulum, chloroplasts, lysosomes, or other eukaryotic membrane-bound organelles**. Energy transduction occurs at the bacterial inner membrane rather than in mitochondria.

## Final acceptance checklist

- [ ] The asset is a genuine 3D rod with rounded poles and inspectable rear, sides, top, bottom, and cut surfaces.
- [ ] The cutaway preserves outer membrane/LPS → periplasm/peptidoglycan → inner membrane → cytoplasm.
- [ ] LPS is associated with the outer leaflet of the outer membrane rather than drawn on the inner membrane or as a separate third membrane.
- [ ] Peptidoglycan is a thin, continuous sacculus inside the periplasm and is visually subordinate to the membrane-to-membrane gap.
- [ ] The nucleoid is not enclosed by a membrane and does not resemble a eukaryotic nucleus or X-shaped chromosomes.
- [ ] Ribosomes are in cytoplasm, not periplasm and not attached to nonexistent rough ER.
- [ ] No eukaryotic membrane-bound organelles are present.
- [ ] Any plasmid is labeled optional and strain-dependent; a plasmid-free state remains valid.
- [ ] Any flagellum, fimbria, or pilus is continuously attached to the envelope, structurally distinguishable, and labeled strain/condition dependent.
- [ ] If both fimbriae and a conjugative pilus are present, the legend distinguishes adhesive and conjugative roles.
- [ ] The UI discloses enlarged membranes, LPS, peptidoglycan mesh, DNA, ribosomes, and appendage diameters as didactic scale exaggerations.
- [ ] Generated turnaround images are marked synthetic hypotheses and are not cited as biological evidence.

## Automatic rejection conditions

Reject the asset if any of the following is observed:

- a billboard, shallow relief, open rear shell, or front-only cutaway presented as a complete 3D cell;
- one membrane only, reversed membrane order, LPS on the inner membrane, or peptidoglycan outside the outer membrane;
- thick Gram-positive-style peptidoglycan or omission of the periplasm;
- a membrane-bounded nucleus, nucleolus, X-shaped eukaryotic chromosomes, mitochondria, Golgi, ER, chloroplasts, lysosomes, or other eukaryotic organelles;
- ribosomes placed in periplasm or on rough ER;
- claims that every *E. coli* cell has plasmids, flagella, fimbriae, conjugative pili, a capsule, a fixed size, or a fixed appendage count;
- a floating appendage or an F/conjugative pilus shown in an explicitly plasmid-free/nonconjugative variant;
- generated imagery used to override the scientific contract or to prove unseen anatomy.

## Limitations and unresolved choices

- This contract describes a generalized species-level teaching archetype rather than a named strain, serotype, medium, motility state, or point in the cell cycle.
- O-antigen length, capsule, extracellular polysaccharides, curli, fimbrial systems, plasmids, flagellar expression, and many envelope proteins vary across strains and conditions. Their omission is acceptable unless a later variant names and supports them.
- A smooth-LPS O-antigen cannot be inferred from generic imagery; some common laboratory strains have rough LPS. The base asset should therefore show generalized LPS without asserting a serotype or exact O-chain.
- Membrane thicknesses, the periplasm, peptidoglycan strands, LPS molecules, DNA, ribosomes, and appendage diameters span incompatible scales. Visible molecular detail must be disclosed as enlarged and schematic.
- Nucleoid conformation and ribosome distribution are dynamic. A cutaway captures one pedagogical arrangement, not a literal universal snapshot.
- Color is pedagogical rather than intrinsic. Do not describe the model colors as the natural visible-light colors of these structures.
- Final scientific sign-off remains pending until the exact geometry, semantic label anchors, optional-feature toggles, cutaway behavior, and eight-view turntable evidence are reviewed.
